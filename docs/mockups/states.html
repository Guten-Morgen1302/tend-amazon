TendState sheet, review artifact

Interaction states

What the person sees in each state. Wording is final copy for the build.

  • Next-dose panel: loading

    Checking...

    The panel keeps its size; text only, no spinner jump.

  • Next-dose panel: empty (nothing due today)

    Nothing due today. Next: tomorrow 8:00 AM.

    No settings link on the kitchen display.

  • Next-dose panel: first run (no schedule ever)

    Waiting for your caregiver to set up reminders.

    Caregiver view shows 'No schedule yet' with the schedule field focused.

  • Server offline (both surfaces)

    Can't reach Tend. Your last saved schedule is shown.

    Banner wins over any 'Saving...'; the button returns to enabled with 'Not saved. Try again.'

  • I took it: already logged

    Already logged at 8:02.

    Debounced 1.5 s after a tap; log_dose is idempotent per slot.

  • Voice failed at runtime

    Voice didn't work here. Type it instead.

    Focus moves to the text field. The mic button stays, text always works.

  • Two doses overdue

    Evening tablet is overdue, due yesterday 9:00 PM. 1 more overdue: Morning tablet, 8:00 AM today.

    Most overdue first; the 'more' line expands to at most 3 rows, each with its own I took it.

  • Schedule proposal (confirm before save)

    Morning tablet, 8:00 AM, every day. Save this schedule?

    Two buttons: Save, Edit. A stale proposal is rejected on save.

  • Alerts: empty

    All quiet. No missed doses.

  • Week strip: partial week

    Mon, Tue taken. Wed: no data. Thu, Fri: due.

    'No data' is never shown as zero.
